How the three classifiers draw the line

Every spot on the plane gets a predicted class, and the shading shows those predictions. The edge between the colours is the decision boundary.

K-nearest neighbours

KNN has no training step. To classify a spot it finds the K closest examples and takes a vote. A small K follows every point, outliers included. A larger K gives a smoother boundary.

Logistic regression

Logistic regression learns one weighted sum of the inputs and passes it through a sigmoid. Its boundary is always a straight line, which is why it fails on the XOR preset. The regularization slider pulls the weights toward zero.

A small neural network

The network adds a hidden layer of tanh units. Each unit draws its own line and the output combines them, so the boundary can bend around XOR. More hidden units give it more flexibility.

Train accuracy only tells you how well a model fits the points it has seen. A model that scores 100% here can still fail on new data.
