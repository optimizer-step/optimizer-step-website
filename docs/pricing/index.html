Pricing questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Email admin@optimizerstep.com.

Is Starter really free for good?

Yes. ML Foundations, its project, the ML Playground and the YouTube lessons stay free for everyone. You only need a free account (your name and email), and never any payment details.

When can I buy a paid tier?

The paid tiers open with the tracks, which open in stages. Founding students hear first, by email. Until then, the best deal is a free founding seat.

Do the prices include taxes?

Yes. The price you see is the price you pay, including all taxes. Nothing is added at checkout.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every tier is a one-time payment, and nothing renews automatically.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, at any time. Everything you have already paid counts towards the higher tier, so you only pay the difference.

What do founding students get?

All Tracks free (it costs ₹1,999 after the founding cohort), a Pro upgrade for ₹1,799 instead of ₹3,000, and 40% off any future paid track.

How do the Pro live sessions work?

Pro includes a live Q&A session every month for 12 months from the date you buy it, plus written feedback on each of your track projects.

What about refunds?

The refund terms will be shown clearly before you pay.
