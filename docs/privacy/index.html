Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Optimizer Step ("we", "us") collects on www.optimizerstep.com, why we collect it and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

Optimizer Step, run by Kalluri Venkatesh, is responsible for your data. For any privacy question or request, email admin@optimizerstep.com.

What we collect

WhenWhat
You claim a founding seat or join the waitlistYour name, email address, chosen track and experience level, the page you signed up on and whether you used a phone or a computer.
You arrive from an ad, email or another websiteThe campaign details in the link (such as utm_source, utm_campaign, or an ad click ID like gclid or fbclid), the website that sent you and the first page you saw. We attach these to your signup so we know which channels bring students.
You use the siteThe Meta Pixel (see "Advertising and analytics tools" below) records the pages you view, actions such as opening the signup form or signing up, the ad that brought you, and device and browser details.
You email usYour email address and whatever you choose to write.

The ML Playground runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or draw in the labs is sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To reserve your founding seat or waitlist place and send your confirmation.
  • To email you about the courses: start dates, access details and course updates.
  • To understand which channels and ads bring students, and to improve the site.
  • To show visitors short notices about recent signups, such as "Founding seat #23 claimed: a Python learner picked NLP & Transformers, 5 minutes ago". They show a seat number, the track, the experience level and roughly how long ago the seat was claimed, never your name or your email. Email us if you would like your seat left out of these notices.

We process your signup details because you ask us to reserve a seat and agree to these uses when you sign up. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see "Your rights").

Where it is stored and who can see it

Signups are stored in Google Sheets in our Google Workspace account, and confirmation emails are sent through Google (Gmail). Only Optimizer Step can see your details. We share data only with the service providers that run these tools for us, or when the law requires it.

Advertising and analytics tools

We use the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, which runs Instagram and Facebook) to measure our Instagram and Facebook ads and show them to people likely to be interested. It receives the pages you view and actions such as signing up, together with your browser and device details. When you sign up, it also receives a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email address and name, so Meta can match the signup to its ads without us sending the plain details. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your Meta ad preferences and in your browser's privacy settings.

Google Analytics, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity are not switched on. If we add them, we will list them here first.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores a few small items in your browser: your light/dark theme choice, the campaign that brought you (for up to 90 days), which signup notices you have already seen or closed (for the current visit only) and, after you sign up, your first name and seat number so the site can recognise you. The Meta Pixel sets its own cookie (_fbp) to recognise your browser between visits. You can clear or block all of this at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your signup details while the founding cohort and the courses you signed up for are running. We delete them sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or stop emailing you (withdraw consent);
  • handle a complaint about how we use your data.

Email admin@optimizerstep.com from the address you signed up with. Every email we send also lets you reply to unsubscribe.

Children

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign up. They can contact us at any time to review or delete your details.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we will also email founding students.
